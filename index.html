le croissant

RECIPE

Simplified Ingredients

Milk: 1 cup (warm)

Instant Yeast: 1 table spoon

Sugar: 3 table spoons

Salt: 1.5 table spoons

Flour: 3 cups (all-purpose)

Butter: 1 cup (2 sticks,cold)

Egg: 1 (for egg wash)

Simple steps

Make Dough: Whisk warm milk, yeast, and sugar. Mix in flour and salt until a dough forms. Knead for 5 mins, cover, and rest for 1 hour.

Prepare Butter: Flatten butter between parchment paper into a rectangle, then chill.

Laminate (The Quick Way): Roll out dough, place butter in center, and fold like a letter. Roll out and fold three times, refrigerating for 20 mins between each fold to keep butter cold.

Shape: Roll dough into a long rectangle ( 1/8 -inch thick). Cut into long, thin triangles. Cut a small notch at the wide end and roll towards the tip.

Proof: Cover croissants and let rise in a warm place for 1–2 hours until puffy.

Bake: Brush with egg wash (egg + 1 tbsp water). Bake at ( 200 ) for 20-25 minutes until deep golden brown.
